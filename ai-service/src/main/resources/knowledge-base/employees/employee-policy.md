# Employee Policy

## Employee Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

Employees are expected to maintain their scheduled working hours unless an approved flexible working arrangement is available.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely when permitted by their department and manager.

Remote work arrangements must follow company security policies.

Employees working remotely must use approved company devices and secure network connections.

## Employee Identification

Every employee is assigned a unique employee ID.

Employee IDs must not be shared with other employees.

## Manager Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for approving leave requests, monitoring team availability, and ensuring employees understand applicable company policies.

## Confidential Information

Employees must not disclose confidential employee information, customer information, financial information, credentials, or internal company information to unauthorized persons.
