# Employee Benefits

## Health Benefits

Eligible employees may receive health insurance benefits according to their employment plan.

Coverage and eligibility depend on the employee's applicable benefits plan.

## Retirement Benefits

Employees may be eligible for retirement benefits according to company policy and applicable employment terms.

## Benefits Questions

Employees should contact the HR or benefits team for questions about:

- Benefits eligibility
- Insurance coverage
- Dependents
- Claims
- Enrollment
- Retirement benefits

The AI assistant should not invent individual employee benefit information.
