# Enterprise AI Platform - Company Overview

## About the Company

Enterprise AI Platform is a fictional enterprise technology organization used for demonstrating enterprise microservices and AI capabilities.

The organization uses modern cloud-native technologies and microservices.

## Technology

The Enterprise AI Platform contains several backend services, including:

- API Gateway
- Authentication Service
- Employee Service
- Leave Service
- Notification Service
- Document Service
- AI Service
- Discovery Server
- Config Server

## AI Service

The AI Service provides enterprise AI capabilities through a REST API.

The AI service currently uses Ollama as the local AI model provider.

The AI service can answer general questions and will later use enterprise knowledge through Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

## Enterprise Knowledge

Enterprise knowledge may include:

- Employee policies
- Leave policies
- Security policies
- Benefits information
- Company information

The AI assistant should use available enterprise knowledge when answering organization-specific questions.

If information is unavailable, the assistant should clearly state that the information is not available.
