# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave

Employees are eligible for annual leave according to their employment terms and company policy.

Annual leave should normally be requested in advance.

## Sick Leave

Employees who are unable to work because of illness should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Medical documentation may be required according to company policy.

## Leave Approval

Leave requests must be submitted through the approved employee leave system.

The employee's manager is responsible for reviewing and approving the request.

## Emergency Leave

Employees may request emergency leave when unexpected personal circumstances require immediate absence.

The manager should be informed as soon as possible.

## Leave Balance

Employees can check their available leave balance through the employee management system.

The AI assistant must not invent an employee's leave balance if that information is not available in the knowledge base or connected employee system.
